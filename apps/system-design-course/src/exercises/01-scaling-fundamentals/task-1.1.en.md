# Assignment 1.1: Quiz — Scaling

## Goal

Test understanding of scaling basics: vertical vs horizontal, stateless vs stateful, scaling cube, Amdahl's Law.

## Requirements

1. Read the Level 1 theory (README)
2. Take a 5-question quiz on key concepts
3. For each question, select the correct answer (one or multiple)
4. Try to answer without looking at the theory

## Checklist

- [ ] Read theory on vertical and horizontal scaling
- [ ] Understand the difference between stateless and stateful services
- [ ] Understand the three axes of the Scaling Cube (X, Y, Z)
- [ ] Familiar with Amdahl's Law and its impact on scaling
- [ ] Complete the quiz with at least 80% score

## How to Check Yourself

1. Open the quiz and answer all questions
2. Review explanations for incorrect answers
3. If score is below 80% — reread the relevant theory sections
