# Task 4.1: Quiz — Databases and Storage

## Objective

Test understanding of database types (SQL vs NoSQL), CAP theorem, ACID vs BASE, sharding, replication, and indexing.

## Requirements

1. Read the Level 4 theory (README)
2. Take a quiz of 5 questions on key concepts
3. For each question, select the correct answer (one or more)
4. Try to answer without looking at the theory

## Checklist

- [ ] Read the theory on SQL vs NoSQL (Document, Key-Value, Column-Family, Graph)
- [ ] Understand the difference between ACID and BASE
- [ ] Understand the CAP theorem and the real CP vs AP choice
- [ ] Familiar with replication (Master-Slave, Master-Master)
- [ ] Understand sharding strategies (Range, Hash, Directory)
- [ ] Familiar with index types (B-tree, Hash, Composite)
- [ ] Understand the role of connection pooling
- [ ] Completed the quiz with a score of at least 80%

## How to Check Yourself

1. Open the quiz and answer all questions
2. Check the explanations for incorrect answers
3. If the score is below 80% — re-read the relevant theory sections
