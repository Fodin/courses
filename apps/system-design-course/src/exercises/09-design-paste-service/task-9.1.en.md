## Objective

Test understanding of key decisions when designing a Paste Service: metadata/content separation, Object Storage, content-addressable storage, CDN caching, and expiration.

## Requirements

1. Read the Level 9 theory (README)
2. Complete the quiz of 4 questions on key concepts
3. For each question, select the correct answer (or multiple)
4. Try to answer without looking at the theory
