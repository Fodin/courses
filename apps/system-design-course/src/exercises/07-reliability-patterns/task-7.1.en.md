## Objective

Test understanding of reliability patterns: Circuit Breaker, retry with backoff, bulkhead, SLO/SLA/SLI, canary releases, and deployment strategies.

## Requirements

1. Read the Level 7 theory (README)
2. Complete the quiz of 5 questions on key concepts
3. For each question, select the correct answer (single or multiple)
4. Try to answer without looking at the theory
